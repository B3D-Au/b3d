End of Year Name Tags

If you are looking for a unique, thoughtful gift for your students or colleagues at the end of the school term, custom name tags make the absolute perfect keepsake. They are light, fun, and add a personal touch to school bags, keys, or desks.

  • Completely Personalized: Give me a list of your classroom names, and I can create unique tags for every single student.
  • Vibrant Colors: Choose bright combinations that kids love, from deep ocean blues to glowing neon tones.
  • Built to Last: Made with sturdy materials that easily survive being tossed around on school backpacks all summer long.

Teaching Tools

Lessons are always more exciting when students can actually hold what they are learning about. I help teachers bring abstract textbook diagrams into physical reality so kids can get hands-on experience during class.

  • Math Visuals: Brightly colored geometric shapes, fraction blocks that fit together perfectly, and tactile puzzles.
  • Science Models: Up-close 3D atom clusters, cell grids, and detailed historical landmarks for history discussions.
  • Classroom Setups: Practical organizational bins, custom desk hooks, or sturdy holder clips to keep the classroom tidy.

Decorative Models

You don't need a school project to get something printed! I print beautiful ornaments, decorative trinkets, desktop toys, and gadgets for your home, office, or classroom shelf.

  • MakerWorld Blueprints: MakerWorld is full of amazing community artists. From articulated animals and light-up moon lamps to intricate holiday decorations, nearly everything on the site can be printed.
  • Simple Requesting: You don't need to know anything about 3D printing. Just browse MakerWorld, send me a link to a design you think looks awesome, and I will take care of the rest.
